\section*{States and basis ordering}
For one spin, choose
\[
\ket{\uparrow}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\ket{\downarrow}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\ket{+}=\frac{\ket{\uparrow}+\ket{\downarrow}}{\sqrt{2}}.
\]
The tensor product uses the ordered basis
$\{\ket{\uparrow\uparrow},\ket{\uparrow\downarrow},
\ket{\downarrow\uparrow},\ket{\downarrow\downarrow}\}$.
Thus the two normalized states in example 01 are
\[
\ket{\psi_{\mathrm{prod}}}=\ket{+}\otimes\ket{\uparrow}
=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\ket{\psi_{\mathrm{Bell}}}
=\frac{\ket{\uparrow\uparrow}+\ket{\downarrow\downarrow}}{\sqrt{2}}
=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Their norm squared is $\sum_{ij}|C_{ij}|^2=1$, computed with
\texttt{np.vdot}, which conjugates its first argument.

\section*{Reshaping and separability}
Write a general state as
\[
\ket{\psi}=\sum_{i,j\in\{\uparrow,\downarrow\}}C_{ij}
\ket{i}\otimes\ket{j}.
\]
With this basis ordering, \texttt{state.reshape(2, 2)} gives
\[
C=\begin{pmatrix}
C_{\uparrow\uparrow}&C_{\uparrow\downarrow}\\
C_{\downarrow\uparrow}&C_{\downarrow\downarrow}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
If $\ket{\psi}=\ket{a}\otimes\ket{b}$, then $C_{ij}=a_i b_j$,
so $C=ab^{\mathsf T}$ has rank one. Conversely, any nonzero rank-one
matrix factors this way and therefore represents a product state.
There is no complex conjugation in this outer product:
it is \texttt{np.outer(a, b)}, not $ab^\dagger$.
For the two examples,
\[
C_{\mathrm{prod}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
C_{\mathrm{Bell}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Their ranks are one and two, respectively: the first state is separable,
whereas the Bell state is entangled. This rank criterion applies to pure
bipartite states.
